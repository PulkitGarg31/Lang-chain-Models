A quiet wind hums through the trees,
Carrying secrets on a gentle breeze.
The sky blushes gold at the edge of day,
While shadows gather, then drift away.

Footsteps fade on a winding road,
Leaving behind each silent load.
Stars awaken, one by one,
Soft as whispers when day is done.

And in the hush of night’s embrace,
The world slows down to find its place.
